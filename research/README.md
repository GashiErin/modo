# Restaurant Website Prospects

Checked 16 October 2026. 50 businesses in Kosovo and 50 in the Republic of Ireland.

- 79 listings showed an Add website control and no website contact field.
- 21 listings linked only to Facebook or Instagram in their website field.
- 73 listings supplied a public business phone number. No private contact details were inferred.
- Each selected listing was opened directly in Google Maps. Marked-closed listings, identified chains, businesses with matching websites found, and uncertain matches were excluded.
- Each selected business also received a name-and-city web search. This is a prospect list, not a guarantee that a business has no website anywhere. Confirm with the business before pitching a website.
- Town / Search Area is the location used to discover the business; the Google Maps Address column in the CSV is the address actually displayed by Maps, including any nearby-town results.
- Website fit is an assessment of the design: priced menus, food photos, hours, directions and enquiries. Cafes need their own food/coffee photos and menu categories. The current reservation and newsletter forms need a connected delivery service before production use.
- Research only: no business was contacted and no outreach messages were sent.
